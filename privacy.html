Privacy Policy — Last updated July 29, 2026

This policy explains what information the ZEL app collects, why, and how it's used. ZEL is a student management app used internally by ZEL (Zone for English Learners) to manage enrollment, attendance, and fee records for its students. It is not a public consumer app — accounts are created by enrolled students and administrators of ZEL.

Information We Collect

When a student registers or an administrator adds a student, we collect:

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Date of birth and gender
  • A profile photo
  • A student ID and, if issued, an NFC card ID used for attendance check-in
  • Attendance records (dates and times marked present/absent)
  • Fee and payment records related to course enrollment
  • Login credentials (your password is never stored in readable form — see "Account Security" below)

How We Use This Information

  • To create and manage your student account and login
  • To track attendance for enrolled classes/batches
  • To manage course fees and payment records
  • To identify students via their profile photo and ID card
  • To communicate with students or guardians about enrollment, attendance, or fees

We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to third parties.

Where Your Data Is Stored

The app is built on Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore Database, and Cloud Functions). Your data is stored and processed on Google's infrastructure, subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We do not use any other third-party analytics or advertising services in this app.

Account Security

Passwords are never stored in a readable form — only a one-way cryptographic hash is kept, managed by Firebase Authentication. Password resets are done either via a secure emailed link, or, for administrators, through a verified in-app process. No ZEL staff member can view a student's password.

Who Can See Your Data

Student records (attendance, fees, personal details) are visible only to that student's own account and to verified ZEL administrators. Administrator access is restricted to specific, individually-authorized accounts.

Data Retention

We retain student records for as long as the student is enrolled, and for a reasonable period afterward for academic and financial record-keeping. You can request deletion of your data at any time (see Contact below), subject to any records we're required to keep for legitimate administrative purposes.

Children's Information

Some ZEL students may be minors. Accounts for minors are created and managed with the involvement of ZEL administrators and, where applicable, a parent or guardian. We only collect the information described above, and only for the purpose of managing enrollment, attendance, and fees.

Your Rights

You can request to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us below. Students can also update most of their own details directly within the app.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
