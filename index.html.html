🛡️ Privacy & Safety

What we collect: name, phone, email, profile photo, batch, attendance records, fee records.

How we use it: only to manage your enrollment, attendance and fees at ZEL. Your data is never shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Who can see it: only you and authorised ZEL admins.

Security: your password is encrypted, all data is transmitted over HTTPS, and stored on Google Firebase secure servers.

Your rights: you can change your password anytime, and you can permanently delete your account using the button below.

⚠️ Delete My Account
Permanently remove your account, photo, attendance and fee records from ZEL. This cannot be undone.
